6. Status and boundaries
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
statically proven:
  - libcuda contains /dev/nvidia-uvm and imports ioctl/open (shell cross-check
    for the string; REA string search had a coverage limitation).
  - The unique ioctl request 0x25 call site is 0x449faf; its helper copies the
    first 16-byte argument into the ioctl payload.
  - Both traced direct callers (0x320b56 and 0x320c8d) pass &device+0xc06 as
    that first argument.
  - cuDeviceGetUuid reads the same device+0xc06 field.
  - faf660 fills the device+0xc06 buffer via an unresolved callback.

UNPROVEN:
  - Target of callback [state+0x30] and ultimate RM/NVML producer.
  - PCI BDF <-> RM gpu_id; whether device+0xc06 is derived from BDF.

coverage limitation:
  - REA izz/axt on this large .so skipped string/xref intervals; objdump and
    strings were used as targeted cross-checks only.
  - The third direct call site to 0x449f40 (0x46f03e) was not traced to its
    UUID source; the runtime callback installed at [holder+0x30] was not
    observed. Neither is an absence proof.

NOT RUN:
  - No ioctl was executed by us; no MMIO, RM request, GPU compute, boot/reboot
    or PR #21. No vendor source, no Ghidra pseudocode, no broad search used as
    proof.
